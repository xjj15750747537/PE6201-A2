## Where the saving comes from

55 cases: **41 bundle** (parallel schedule is 1 turn shorter — these are the cases whose sequential trace reaches `lookup_patient`, i.e. everything except an immediate criteria-stage exit); **14 do not bundle** (sequential and parallel schedules are identical turn-for-turn). The 14 non-bundling cases are the ones that escalate or ask before ever looking up the patient: red-flag terms, specialty mismatch, a missing mandatory test, and the four prompt-injection cases.

This means the saving is not uniform across the evaluation set — it is exactly the cases that get past the criteria check that benefit, which is itself evidence that the dependency rule is doing real work rather than being applied mechanically everywhere.
