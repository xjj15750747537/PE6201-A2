## Where the saving comes from

55 cases: **41 bundle** (parallel schedule is shorter — everything except
an immediate criteria-stage exit, plus REF-5602 which bundles two ways);
**14 do not bundle** (sequential and parallel schedules are identical
turn-for-turn — the cases that escalate or ask before ever looking up the
patient: red-flag terms, specialty mismatch, a missing mandatory test, and
the four prompt-injection cases).

## Methodology

Both schedules are executed independently from a clean observation set,
through `tools.call()`, for every one of the 55 cases — not just
REF-5602. For the 54 non-REF-5602 cases, `build_script()` still supplies
the call list and its already-correct, dependency-aware arguments (so
this script cannot silently disagree with the harness about WHAT to
call), but the final decision record is recomputed independently from the
re-executed observations rather than taken from `build_script()`'s own
output. This means the reported cross-schedule match is a genuine result
of running both paths, not an artefact of comparing one value to itself.
